Aurora Glass

A luminous study of flowing transparent glass, soft reflections and elegant sculptural movement.

About this artwork

Aurora Glass explores the visual beauty of transparent glass through flowing forms, layered reflections and a luminous atmosphere. The composition is designed around clarity, depth and graceful movement.

This project represents a futuristic 3D art direction from Usman Creative Studio, created for viewers who appreciate refined abstract visuals and contemporary digital design.

Creative direction

Style: Transparent glass, abstract 3D forms and luminous reflections.

Visual mood: Elegant, futuristic and atmospheric.
