Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Usman Creative Studio respects your privacy. This page explains what information may be collected when you submit a project inquiry through this website and how that information is used.

Information you provide

When you use the project inquiry form, you may provide your name, email address, requested service, budget, preferred delivery date and project details. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive personal information in the form.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit to receive, review and respond to your project inquiry, communicate with you about the requested work, and manage the inquiry. The form is configured to send the inquiry to the studio and to send an automated acknowledgement to the email address you provide.

Service providers

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Project inquiries are processed using Web3Forms, and automated acknowledgement emails are sent using EmailJS. These providers may process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy terms and practices. Please review their policies for further details.

Sharing and selling

Usman Creative Studio does not sell your submitted inquiry information. Information may be handled by the service providers described above to operate the website form and email process, or disclosed where required by law.

Retention and your choices

We keep inquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond to and manage the inquiry, maintain relevant project records, or meet applicable legal obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, email uu482035@gmail.com. We may need to retain limited information where legally required.

Security

We use third-party services to process form submissions and email. No method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid sending confidential or highly sensitive information through the form.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its inquiry process changes. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact uu482035@gmail.com.
